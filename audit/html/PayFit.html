Get Your Questions Answered

  • What exactly does PayFit do?

    At PayFit, we are revolutionizing payroll and HR management with our innovative software solution for SMBs. We are not just simplifying payroll—we are automating HR processes and empowering businesses and their employees to grow together.

  • What values does PayFit prioritize in its workplace culture?


    At PayFit, our values are the foundation of everything we do. They guide our innovative payroll and HR solutions, shape our relationships with clients, and define the experience of every team member. Our core values are:

    • Care: Respect and support for each other.
    • Excellence: Embracing a growth mindset.
    • Humility: Treating everyone equally.
    • Passion: Driving PayFit’s success.


  • Why do we have behaviours at PayFit?

    To ensure our values are actively practiced, we follow six key behaviours:

    • 👩🏽‍🤝‍👨🏼 Customer first: Before my project, my team, and my comfort zone.

    • 🔧 Act now: When something matters, I do it today. I am solution-oriented so I take calculated risks rather than wait for certainty. I fail fast.

    • 🎯 Own delivery: I take full responsibility for my results. I challenge the status quo and act in the interest of the whole company.

    • 🤝 Disagree and Commit: I engage and express my opinions. Once decisions are made, I support them.

    • 🏆 Cut through: I contribute to our collective efficiency by removing complexity before adding anything new.

    • 🔎 Grow everyday: I actively develop my craft and shape how I work in the AI era.

    These behaviours integrate our values into every aspect of work, creating a thriving, supportive culture.

  • What benefits does PayFit offer to its employees?

    • Flexibility: Work from Anywhere policy allows you to work from any location within France, Spain, or the UK.
    • Learning & Development: Access to a learning platform for daily skill development and English language courses to improve business communication.
    • Career Development: Encouragement for open discussions with managers and HR to explore career progression and internal moves.
    • Health & Wellness: Health insurance, free mental health support through Moka.Care, and gym memberships at preferential rates.
    • Parental Support: Four extra weeks of parental leave beyond the legal requirement, supported by PayFit.

  • How does PayFit support career development and growth?

    PayFit supports career development through continuous learning opportunities, mentorship programs, and clear career progression paths. We encourage employees to pursue their professional goals and provide resources to help them succeed.

  • What should I expect during the interview process?

    Our recruitment process includes several steps. 

    1) First, a discovery call with a Talent Acquisition Specialist to assess your fit with the role and our way of working. 

    2) Next, you'll meet your potential manager to discuss your vision, challenges, and integration into the team. 

    3) You'll then complete a case study or live exercise where your results and reasoning will be evaluated. 

    4) Optionally, you might meet with a director to reflect on your interest in joining our team. 

    Do you feel like joining our rocketship?

  • What is the expected duration for the application process?

    Although application timelines can vary due to various factors, our goal is to inform you within 7 days if we’ll be moving forward with your application. From there, our recruiters will let you know during the phone interview what to expect and will provide updates on timing throughout the process.

  • What can I expect during the onboarding process at PayFit?

    Our onboarding process is designed to welcome new hires and integrate them into our culture. We start with a welcome breakfast, assign you a buddy for support, and provide orientation sessions, including training on our tools and processes. You'll meet team members, key stakeholders, and attend sessions with department  to learn about their missions. Additionally, you'll participate in global onboarding sessions to connect with various leaders across the company. 

    This ensures you feel welcomed, informed, and ready to start.

  • Do PayFit have an inclusive hiring process?

    Yes, at PayFit, we are committed to inclusivity at every stage of our hiring process. We use inclusive language in job descriptions, conduct interviews thoughtfully, and implement various strategies to promote diversity.

  • What initiatives does PayFit have for diversity, inclusion, and belonging?

    Our strategy for diversity and inclusion (D&I) focuses on enhancing team diversity and nurturing an inclusive culture. Some of our key actions include:

    • 🌱 Empowering Inclusive Leaders: Equipping all managers with the skills to lead inclusively.
    • 🤝 Welcoming New Hires with D&I: Introducing all new employees to our D&I values and practices.
    • ⚙️ Embedding D&I into Our Core: Integrating D&I principles into our key people processes.
    • ⭐️ Supporting ERGs: We actively support Employee Resource Groups (ERGs) like Women at PayFit and LGBTQIA+ Pride@PayFit. Within Women at PayFit, we have developed a mentorship program to further support and empower our employees.

Get to know us


Do you want to help us shape what the future of work will look like and how it will best embrace our life's aspirations? If this sounds like a journey you want to embark on, we may have the right role for you! PayFit is an intuitive software as a service payroll and HRIS solution designed specifically for SMB. Since 2015, we have set ourselves a mission to simplify payroll for SMBs and enable employers and employees to grow together. We are a European company operating from 3 main countries (France, Spain, and the UK) where we are supporting over 20,000 clients.

Creating a fulfilling work environment and culture is also a core mission at PayFit, and our day-to-day work philosophy is reflected in our four values:

Care: We genuinely care for others whoever they are, whatever they think.

Excellence: We aim to improve and achieve better results every day.

Humility: Staying humble and exchanging transparent feedback helps us to grow and improve.

Passion: We are the architects of PayFit's success.

A key part of our culture, and essential for our success, is also improving the diversity of our teams and building an inclusive culture where you can be yourself at work.

This is why our recruitment focuses on the skills you demonstrate, and not only on your academic background or previous professional experiences. At PayFit we understand that you can gain applicable skills through a variety of life experiences and we are interested in knowing them, too.

Location

The position is based in Carrer de Pamplona, 104, 08018 Barcelona, in our offices ➡️ http://bit.ly/4kjOhbE

What will be your role?

Get onboard of the PayFit rocket by joining our UK Sales Team as a Sales Development Respresentative!
Your mission will be to: Generate outbound selling opportunities for Payfit UK.

You will be responsible for:

As a Business Developer / Sales Development Representative, you will play a key role in contributing to PayFit’s growth.

Your main responsibility will be to generate as many new business opportunities as possible through 100% outbound prospecting.
Identifying, qualifying prospects (30% by email and 70% by phone), and engaging them will be at the heart of your day-to-day work.

More details:

  • Prospecting and building a sales pipeline to generate qualified leads for our Account Executives.

  • Understanding your prospects’ needs and presenting them with the most suitable solution.

  • Identifying key decision-makers and influencers, such as CEOs, Office Managers, Payroll Managers, CFOs and Founders.

  • Working closely with your Sales Manager to develop your pipeline and achieve your monthly targets.

  • Keeping clear and relevant information about potential customers up to date in Salesforce, our CRM.

  • Proactively sharing ideas, best practices and feedback with your team to help improve PayFit’s processes.

This role could be a great fit for you if

  • You are completely fluent in English

  • You are organised, structured and able to communicate clearly and concisely.

  • You have plenty of energy and use it to engage and convince potential customers.

  • You are naturally curious and always keen to learn more.

Interview process :

1️⃣ Film a short video and get creative

2️⃣ Interview with a recruiter - 30 min

3️⃣ Business case with Daniel, Manager (30/40 min)

You will meet your future manager and discuss the role in more detail. The interview will include a role-play exercise, as well as a conversation about your future responsibilities, personality, expectations and the way we work.

& Interview 2 with Megan, Head of Sales UK (30mn)

👉 Background Check (ISO27001 certification): identity, diploma, and past professional experiences will be checked after the offer.

What we offer

Flexibility: We believe it is key to producing your best work and being fulfilled. We therefore offer the possibility to work away from our main offices, within Spain, as well as abroad, for a defined period. Further requirements may apply depending on the role and your overall experience.

Learning & Development: At PayFit, we offer a comprehensive learning platform that enables you to acquire new skills daily, supported by our company. We also have English language courses to improve your business communication vocabulary and get to the next level.

Career Development: We want you to progress and be free to choose which direction you want to grow. There are also opportunities for internal moves.

Health promotion: Gym subscription (Urban Sports Club) with a preferential rate.

Meals: 6 EUR lunch voucher per workday with our partner Edenred.

Transportation: 50 % of public transport card is covered by PayFit .

Health Insurance: Full coverage on private insurance with Alan.

Parental support program: 17 weeks of leave for a mother and a co-parent, paid at 100%. Support with a nursery for your child.

Home office budget: Yearly work from home budget to help you get set up in the best conditions. A MacBook is our standard working tool.

Time off: 25 days of holidays per year.

Disability Inclusion: All of our positions are open to any person living with a disability. To guarantee equal treatment and opportunities, we will take, based on individual needs, appropriate measures to adapt the work conditions of PayFiters with disabilities, and if needed also during the recruitment process. Please let us know what you need and we will do our best to accommodate!
